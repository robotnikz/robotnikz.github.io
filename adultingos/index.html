Die kostenlose Version der App enthält Werbebanner, die durch Google AdMob (Google LLC, 1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA) bereitgestellt werden. Google kann dabei gerätebezogene Kennungen und Nutzungsdaten verarbeiten, um relevante Werbung anzuzeigen.

Der optionale Kauf zum Entfernen der Werbung wird vollständig über Apple StoreKit abgewickelt. Der Entwickler erhält dabei keine Zahlungsdaten. Es gelten die Datenschutzrichtlinien von Apple.

5. Benachrichtigungen

Die App kann optionale lokale Push-Benachrichtigungen senden (z. B. Fristen-Erinnerungen). Diese werden ausschließlich lokal auf deinem Gerät verarbeitet und nicht an Server übertragen. Du kannst Benachrichtigungen jederzeit in den iPhone-Einstellungen deaktivieren.

Privacy Policy

2. No personal data collected

AdultingOS does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data on its own. There is no user account, no server, and no analytics by the developer. All content and progress data is stored exclusively on your device.

3. Advertising – Google AdMob

The free version of the app displays banner ads provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC, 1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA). Google may process device identifiers and usage data to show relevant ads.

On first launch, the app asks for your consent via the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) dialog. If you decline, only non-personalized ads are shown. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy → Tracking.

Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

4. In-App Purchase – "Remove Ads"

The optional purchase to remove ads is handled entirely through Apple StoreKit. The developer does not receive any payment information. Apple's Privacy Policy applies.

5. Notifications

The app may send optional local push notifications (e.g. deadline reminders). These are processed exclusively on your device and never transmitted to any server. You can disable notifications at any time in iPhone Settings.

6. Your rights (GDPR)

Since we do not process personal data ourselves, rights of access, rectification, and erasure do not apply to the developer. For data processed by Google or Apple, please contact those providers directly.

Contact: tobiashalbei@mailbox.org

We reserve the right to update this privacy policy as needed. The current version is always available on this page.
